Defend the Unicorn Castle against endless waves of magical invaders in this compact yet surprisingly deep tower defense experience.

Build enchanted unicorn-powered towers, combine slowing magic, area attacks and devastating boss-killing power, then adapt your strategy to a newly generated battlefield every game.

Early enemies wander through the maze in confusion, but over time they learn faster routes to your castle. Rare mutant creatures may emerge, immune to slowing magic and capable of breaking even the strongest defenses.

Featuring procedurally generated maps, adaptive enemy behavior, recurring boss battles, endless progression, particle-filled rainbow magic and a dark fantasy soundtrack generated entirely in real time using Web Audio.

How long can your castle survive?

Controls:
1-4 = Select Tower
5 = Boost
← → = Change Selection

Place towers on empty tiles, press Start Wave, earn Magic, upgrade defenses and survive as long as possible.
